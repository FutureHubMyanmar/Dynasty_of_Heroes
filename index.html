The legend begins

A prophecy.

A promise.

In the kingdom of Pagan, an earthquake is followed by a prophecy: a boy born that night will one day inherit the throne. A search begins, and the infant Mg Kyan is carried into hiding.

The child grows up beside Mg Kan, the general's grandson. They are not brothers by birth — but the bond between them will shape the fate of a kingdom.

“If I don't watch your back, who will?”
